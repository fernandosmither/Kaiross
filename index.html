Our programs

STALL

The largest research fellowship focused on risks from advanced AI. The research is forthcoming.

A part-time, remote research fellowship matching aspiring AI safety researchers with experts in the field. Matching is ongoing.

Research in this area has been accepted at ICML and NeurIPS. Not ours, but it has been accepted, and we consider that encouraging.

Our Spring 2026 round featured 137 projects and 427 mentees, our largest round yet. A round of follow-ups is planned.

Pathwanderer

Mentorship and financial support for university clubs focused on AI safety and policy

A fellowship providing funding, mentorship, community, and other resources to organizers of AI safety student groups. Funds are released once organizers can demonstrate they no longer need them.

Pathwanderer enables organizers to run ambitious programming next semester.

Our Spring 2026 round featured 69 Pathwanderer Fellows at 50 universities across 12 countries, none of whom have heard from us since.

Procrastinator Residency

A three-month residency considering the infrastructure and capacity the AI safety ecosystem needs.

For highly agentic executors from any background (operations, engineering, policy, design, or writing), transitioning them into highly deliberative observers with mentorship and placement support.

Hosted in-person at the Consternation office in Berkeley, June 15 – August 28, 2026, with a $6,000/month stipend that vests upon completion of the following residency.

Run in partnership with Consternation. Our inaugural cohort of 27 residents was selected from roughly 1,500 applications. The remaining 1,473 will be notified.

Local Trivialities Project

Intensive three-day workshops on how to think about AI safety and biosecurity later.

Residential workshops for 20–35 attendees featuring foundational lectures, guest speakers, and 1:1 career conversations, all of which can be rescheduled.

Open to applicants worldwide, with travel support available upon return.

Our first workshop under Chronos ran in Oxford in June 2026, earning a 9.5/10 likelihood-to-attend-the-next-one, which has not been scheduled.

About us

We're a nonprofit focused on decelerating talent into the fields of AI safety and policy. We believe the development of transformative AI will be one of the most consequential events in history. Getting it right requires building a robust ecosystem of researchers, policymakers, technical professionals, and skilled operators, and we look forward to seeing one.

We operate without urgency. If transformative AI arrives soon, that is a matter for the version of us that exists then. For us, that means staying lean, shipping eventually, and constantly reassessing whether our priorities are the right ones. The reassessment is ongoing and has itself been reassessed twice.

Our current strategy focuses on interventions that can stall flexibly in response to public interest, allowing the fields of AI safety and policy to absorb talent at a rate we consider sustainable. We execute on this strategy by facilitating scalable research postponement through STALL, supporting decentralized community deferral at universities through Pathwanderer, holding the ecosystem's operational capacity in reserve through the Procrastinator Residency, and introducing students to AI safety and biosecurity through the Local Trivialities Project, from a respectful distance.

Since 2023, we've supported over 1,200 professionals worldwide. Our alumni continue to work on critical challenges at the organisations where we found them.

Our name comes from the Greek. Kairos is the opportune moment — the critical window in which action changes everything. Chronos is the time on either side of it. There is considerably more of it.
